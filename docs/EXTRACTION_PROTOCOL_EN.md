# English guide to the frozen extraction protocol

[Overview](../README.md) · [Historical Spanish protocol](FROZEN_EXTRACTION_PROTOCOL.txt) · [Data dictionary](DATA_DICTIONARY.md)

This reader guide summarizes the protocol frozen on 24 October 2026. It is not a new preregistration and does not recast exploratory work as confirmatory research. The original file remains unchanged. Current provenance display rules are explained separately in [Interpretation](INTERPRETATION.md).

## Frame and eligibility

The sampling frame is Table 2 of [Kaissar et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/fi18050234), comprising 25 entries previously selected by that review, including quality screening. Eligibility requires a published empirical intrusion-detection article evaluating benign and attack traffic with a quantum computing component, whether simulated or physical. Quantum-inspired classical optimization alone is excluded. Eligibility is independent of the sign of results, confusion-matrix availability or full-text access.

The initial five pilot records were retained and the remaining frame entries screened. The final accounting is 21 included articles, two excluded and two with unresolved eligibility. Related reports are linked into 20 identified documentary families among included articles. Detector configurations, points and administrative rows are not independent studies. External methodological examples remain outside the denominator.

## Access and extraction

Keep complete/substantive methodology access, author versions, abstracts, failed retrieval and confirmed access restrictions distinct. An HTTP failure alone does not establish a publisher paywall or absent reporting. Inaccessible studies are not replaced.

For each article–source version–dataset/subset–detector–operating point, retain:

- The original claim, source location, evaluated unit and dataset name.
- Class meanings, matrix axes, denominators, counts or original metrics.
- Threshold/decision-rule selection, evaluation set and aggregation procedure.
- Any correspondence or ambiguity between these fields.

FPR and TPR must refer to the same evaluated point. Rounded summaries, normalized matrices, averages and interpolated ROC points are not converted into invented integer counts. A nominal split does not identify the class denominator of another source object.

## Analysis and checks

Separate observed rate, empirical resolution, conditional sampling uncertainty and supported calibration guarantees. The conventional one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson bound assumes independent observations and a detector/threshold selected independently of the evaluation sample. Reference levels 1%, 0.1% and 0.01% belong to this analysis, not to the original authors unless explicitly stated.

Preserve interpretable quantum–classical comparisons without requiring them for eligibility. No pooling, simultaneous coverage claim or field-wide frequency estimate is introduced. Technical source re-reading and independent binomial-tail inversion check decisive fields. They do not constitute independent human duplicate extraction. Authors have confirmed completion of human validation; [review disclosure](AI_AND_REVIEW.txt) states its documented scope.

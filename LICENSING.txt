LICENSING SCOPE — QML-IDS Operating Points, v2.1.0

Software (scripts/, requirements-tested.txt): MIT, full text in LICENSE.
Derived aggregate records (data/), documentation (docs/, README.txt and metadata),
and regenerated tables/figures: CC BY 4.0, terms and legal-code link in LICENSE-DATA.
These are distinct scopes, not a requirement to dual-license every file.
Facts and bibliographic metadata retain attribution; no exclusive rights are claimed
in the published observations transcribed from the sources. Third-party rights are
not relicensed. No primary reports, full texts, source artwork, source notebooks,
MDPI template, editorial PDFs, internal review sheets or credentials are included.

This follows the thesis practice of permissive code licensing (MIT in VBC and the
certification-cost artifact) and a separate CC BY 4.0 scope for derived evidence
and documentation (the hybrid-integrity artifact). It does not copy a manuscript
licence or another article's grant information.

The repository and a fixed-version Zenodo archive are the intended dissemination
route. A draft/reserved DOI is not a published record. Actual public identifiers
are added only after they exist, with the precise status indicated.
